A consistent operating model

From patient need to reviewed action

  1. 1
    Understand

    Use entered notes, completed transcripts, or live utterances.

  2. 2
    Ground

    Consult approved policy, Knowledge, and accessible records.

  3. 3
    Recommend

    Suggest guidance, routing, missing details, or record updates.

  4. 4
    Confirm

    The Access Center agent checks, corrects, and approves the action.

Design principles

Assist the agent. Do not replace their judgment.

Every experience should make the source, uncertainty, and required human action clear.

01

Show the source

Link recommendations back to the approved policy or Salesforce record.

02

Ask before writing

Require review before creating Cases, Tasks, or handoff summaries.

03

Respect access

Never expose patient or operational data beyond the user’s permissions.

04

Recover gracefully

Make it easy to correct a suggestion or continue without AI assistance.
